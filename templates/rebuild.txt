Lines: each one short checkable sentence of one kind: proof (re-runnable: rerun has chain and block; fact has a plain name such as Seat supply, value and unit), statement (per the statement rule below) or claim (someone else said it); fill by for statements and claims. The record has no line or claim limit. Never drop an earlier line to save space or because it matters less. The only reasons to drop are: not about the subject, or replaced by a newer statement from the same speaker. Merge lines that say the same thing into ONE line, even across files, speakers or dates; it lists every origin in from and speaker in by, counts as carried, and its origins stay out of dropped.
